How photo search works

  1. You take a selfie.
  2. It's compared with the faces in this event's photos.Only this event's photos. Nothing else is searched.
  3. You see the photos you're in.
  4. Your selfie is thrown away.Nothing about it is kept afterwards.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't know who you are. There are no names or profiles here, only photos.
  • Your selfie isn't stored, shared, or used for anything else.

Matching isn't perfect. You might miss a photo where your face is small, turned away or covered, and once in a while you might see one that isn't you.
